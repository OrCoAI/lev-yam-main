## Context

The plan drafted five "pillars": the village and the sea · teams by the sea · private celebrations
· the kitchen · community and יוזמות. Some were subjects (the sea, the kitchen) and some were
audiences (teams, celebrations), so a post could sit in two pillars or in none, and "balance the
pillars" had no single meaning. The owner asked for a clear definition before the first pack.

## Decision

1. **A theme is an audience.** Each theme names who the post speaks to, the service from
   `FACTS.md` §שירותים it leads to, and the one action it asks for. Every post has exactly one
   theme — "who is this for?" has one answer.

   | Theme | Who | Service (`FACTS.md`) | The action | Share |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | **Companies & teams** | HR, team leads, managers | business events — team days, strategy days, offsites | WhatsApp inquiry for a business event | **45%** |
   | **Private hosts** | people planning a celebration | private events | WhatsApp inquiry for a private event | **25%** |
   | **Space renters** | facilitators and organizers | renting the whole venue — workshop, retreat, longer stays | inquiry to rent | **15%** |
   | **Weekend visitors & community** | individuals, couples, families; the Sunday circle; initiatives | weekends · community Sunday · יוזמות | come Fri/Sat, join, share | **15%** |

2. **The shares are per month, per channel**, not per pack — a week may lean one way, the month
   evens out. The monthly audit reports the actual split against this table.
3. **The place is the setting of every post, never a theme of its own.** The village, the sea and
   the kitchen appear inside every theme (a team on the sand, a birthday table with the fish). No
   post is "just a view": a village story goes out under the audience it serves best — usually
   Companies & teams (a fishing-village tour for a team).
4. **Changing a share or a theme is a PR to this table** (the plan's evolve rule: the monthly audit
   proposes, the owner merges) — never a drift inside a weekly pack.

## Consequences

- `content-week` (PR C) picks a week's posts against this table and tags each draft with its
  theme; the post record carries the theme, so the split is a query over `content/posts/`.
- Every draft ends in its theme's action; a post without one is incomplete, not "brand awareness".
- Business first is a bet: if the outcome check shows inquiries come from private hosts instead,
  the shares move — that is evidence, and it changes this table, not the definition.
- Future channels inherit the table; a channel that serves one audience only (LinkedIn → Companies)
  may run a single theme without an amendment.
