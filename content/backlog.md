# Backlog — shaped idea cards

`Ideas/00 Raw`; a card is shaped when it names its audience (ADR 0070), the one action, and what
it needs. Rules: [README.md](README.md). Cards not picked for 8 weeks move to the archive list at
the bottom; the backlog never holds fewer than 10 shaped cards (the plan's evolve rule §5).

The card format: [README.md §A card](README.md#a-card--backlogmd).
