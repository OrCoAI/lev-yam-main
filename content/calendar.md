What the season gives the next packs. Claude adds hooks at the Sunday session; the monthly audit
drops the ones that passed. A date-bound post carries `expires` (README §A post record). Facts
about any date (opening hours, an event) come from `FACTS.md` or `/app/events`, never from here.
