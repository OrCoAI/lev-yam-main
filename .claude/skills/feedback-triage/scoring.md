# feedback-triage — scoring

Score = **frequency** (1–5: one voice → most sources) × **impact** (1–5: cosmetic → blocks a
circle's core job or a current-block outcome metric) × **fit** (0.5 / 1 / 1.5: contradicts a
principle / neutral / directly serves a VISION principle) − **cost class** (0 small fix on a
live module · 2 new UI surface · 4 new schema/permissions or spine change · 6 new module).

Frequency × impact matrix:
| | Low impact | High impact |
|---|---|---|
| **High frequency** | quality-of-life batch (module logs) | **top priority** |
| **Low frequency** | note, deprioritize | important for a segment — verify with 2 more voices |

Rules:
- A single loud voice is frequency 1 whatever its volume.
- "Impact" is measured against the current block's outcome metrics first, the vision second.
- Anything that needs schema, permissions or the events/finance spine is an initiative
  (feature-spec), never a module-log line — say so in the table.
- Prices, customer names, phone numbers never appear in the digest (public repo).
